Service details

Software maintenance is proactive and includes scheduled activities to address non-urgent issues or perform planned improvements. Software support is reactive and covers activities on handling urgent issues that can hamper software operation and cause downtimes. Most of our clients opt for both types to ensure stability, scalability, high performance, and robust security of software in the long run.

From the day your software system goes live, you'll be faced with the need to start supporting, maintaining, and properly "evolving" your application. Our IT support and maintenance services are designed to proactively handle the maintenance of software to ensure it is bug-free at deployment. We follow the best application software maintenance practices to make your software robust and secure.
